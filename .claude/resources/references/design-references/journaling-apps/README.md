# Journaling / mood apps (Reflect page inspiration)

No assets saved (copyrighted). Notes from web research, 2026-11-11; sources are reviews and design portals, not hands-on tests.

## Apple Journal
- Source: https://thesweetsetup.com/our-first-look-at-apples-new-journal-app/ , https://www.macstories.net/reviews/apples-journal-app-journaling-for-all/
- Does well: one big "New Entry" button, no feed pressure, entries as cards on a softly colored background, prompts to get past the blank page.
- Ebb takes: the single entry point (our brain dump pill), soft card-on-color past entries. Leaves: algorithmic suggestions (user agency, wording rules).

## Daylio / How We Feel
- Does well: tap-to-log, almost no writing, icon-based, no gamification.
- Ebb takes: tap-first entry points; insight screens that are glanceable.

## Mood-insight design patterns
- Source: https://peri-design-portal.netlify.app/components/insight-cards
- Overview (range tabs, stat row, one summary sentence) above a "playbook" of personal patterns; only confident patterns counted as insights.
- Progressive unlock (MoodKit): deeper insight appears as data grows; sparse-data screens say "a few more check-ins and patterns show up".
- Ebb takes: one warm summary sentence on top, patterns appear only when real, empty state is encouraging, never a streak.

## Finch
- Gamified pet; heavy by design. Ebb already has the mascot, so skip streaks/pet-care loops (no guilt rule).

## Google Health (ex-Fitbit app, 5.0, May 2026)
- Source: https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/google-health/google-health-app/ , https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-google-health-fitbit-app-ai-coach-widget/
- Reported: four tabs (Today, Fitness, Sleep, Health), tap a metric to open its stats page, customizable dashboards, badges dropped. Searches gave little on chart styling, so only the structure is borrowed.
- Ebb takes: one metric per card (title, one friendly line, small chart), tap-through detail, modular cards. Leaves: AI coach, raw numbers.
