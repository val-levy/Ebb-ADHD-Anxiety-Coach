# Clay (clay.com): branding inspiration

Source: https://www.clay.com/ (screenshots provided by the user, 2026-11-10). Copyrighted: never ship these assets or copy their logo, objects or illustrations.

## What they do well
- **Claymation world:** hand-made-looking 3D clay objects sit in a soft landscape (grainy blue sky, puffy clouds, rolling green hills, simple cone trees).
- **Film grain/noise** over flat colors makes the digital surfaces feel tactile and analog.
- **Clean UI on top:** white panels with big soft corners, crisp near-black type, small uppercase gray section labels with letter spacing.
- **Icon tiles:** each item gets a colorful, chunky, multi-color 3D-ish icon inside a pale gray rounded-square tile.
- Palette: sky blue, grass greens, teal, clay orange, cobalt blue, butter yellow, pink accents.

## What Ebb takes
- A calm landscape backdrop (sky + hills + grain) at the top of tab screens. Hills and outdoors suit a calming app.
- White rounded panels + crisp type + small uppercase section labels.
- Colorful multi-tone icons in pale rounded tiles for every row.
- Grain texture on colored surfaces.

## What Ebb doesn't take
- Their objects, logo, or exact scenes. Ebb draws its own simple hills/trees in code.
