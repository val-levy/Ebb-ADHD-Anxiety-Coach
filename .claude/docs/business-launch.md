# Business and launch

Status: **not started** (accounts in progress) · Source: product-spec.md, "Business model", "Setbacks" · Master: [roadmap.md](roadmap.md)

## Pricing (not built)
- Free tier: on-demand resets and the weekly insight.
- Lifetime unlock (~$25–40): guard windows, personalization, medication baselines. Needs StoreKit 2.
- Later add-on: therapist export and Bluetooth sensor mode.

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Paid Apple Developer Program membership (also unblocks Sign in with Apple; see accounts plan).
- [ ] App Store listing and privacy label follow [wording-and-safety.md](wording-and-safety.md) and match what actually leaves the phone (see roadmap.md "Privacy decision").
- [ ] Lawyer reviews [wording-and-safety.md](wording-and-safety.md) and the App Store listing before launch (FDA General Wellness guidance, App Store 1.4.1).
- [ ] Disclaimer in onboarding and Terms: "Ebb is a wellness tool, not medical advice. It doesn't diagnose or treat any condition."
- [ ] App Review notes explaining the workout-session use; a fallback build without long sessions.
- [ ] Crisis path reviewed (988 call/text is built).
- [ ] Recruit 10–20 beta users from ADHD communities; TestFlight.
- [ ] Account deletion live (Apple requirement; in the accounts plan).

## Positioning
"The calm button that presses itself." Lead with design built for busy, ADHD-shaped minds and privacy; never claim to treat or manage a condition (see wording-and-safety.md). Channels: ADHD Reddit/Discord,
coaches and therapists, privacy-focused tech press.

## Success metrics (starting guesses)
Helped 50%+ · false alarms under 1/day · 40%+ active after 4 weeks · 3+ on-demand taps/week · under 5% Watch battery
per guard-window hour.
